FAQ

Questions we answer out loud

When can I buy or install it?

Public product target is Fall / Winter 2026. Join the waitlist for first notice. Workshop builds already exist for internal and pilot work; that is not the same as a polished public installer.

Is this cloud AI writing?

The core path is local first via Ollama on your machine. Your manuscripts are files you keep. We will not market a default path that uploads your novel for training.

Will it write a whole novel in five minutes?

No. Full length runs can take hours depending on model size, chapter count, and hardware. Patience is part of the product honesty.

Do I still need to edit?

Yes. Novelmate Studio assists with structure and drafting. You set vision and keep final say. Human taste is not optional.

What formats leave the studio?

Markdown for continued craft. EPUB and DOCX when you export. Exact packaging options will be listed in release notes at launch.

How do you prove claims?

Phyllux Technologies tracks this lane on research status row five. Read that table beside any marketing sentence.

Where are books made with this stack?

Sproule Lit titles on phyllux.com are part of the same production story: human direction, machine assistance, local custody.
