FAQ

Short answers. This is not legal advice.

Does Novelmate Studio run in the cloud?

The product direction is local first: your hardware, your models where you configure them, your files. A future hosted tier would be a separate announcement with its own security story.

How long does a full book take?

It depends on model size, GPU, and settings. Plan for hours on a laptop class machine for long jobs, not seconds. Thermal limits and VRAM matter in the real world.

AI and copyright

Disclosure rules vary by region and platform. Keep human review in the loop for anything you publish or sell. For depth, see the application documentation on AI authorship and copyright in the Content Studio source tree (COPYRIGHT_AND_AI_AUTHORSHIP.md).

What you should not expect

Guaranteed bestseller outcomes, unlimited instant generation at scale, or “we own none of your risk” hand waves. If a claim is not measured on your setup, treat it as marketing from someone else.
