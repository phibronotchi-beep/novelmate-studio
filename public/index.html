What it is

A studio for finishing books, not chasing dashboards

Authors get tired. Motor issues happen. Blank pages win too often. Novelmate Studio sits beside you as a structured companion: you set the vision, the machine helps with drafting, and you keep final say.

Premise in. Manuscript out.

Start with a premise, outline notes, and constraints. Move through scene plans into prose. Keep entity ledgers and summaries so later chapters still match earlier ones.

  • Outline and beat structure you can edit
  • Scene by scene drafting with drift checks
  • Developmental, line, and copy edit passes when you ask for them
  • Markdown first exports, plus EPUB and DOCX when you are ready

Local first by design

Generation runs through Ollama on hardware you control. Your drafts are files on disk. We do not sell a story where your manuscript becomes someone else’s training set by default.

  • No required cloud manuscript vault for the core path
  • Works with the models you install locally
  • Honest about heat, memory, and queue time

The pipeline

Five stages from blank page to shelf ready files

Same story spine whether you prefer the web studio, the classic tabs, or the CLI. Pick the path that fits your day.

Plan

Premise, outline, genre voice, and hard constraints. The map before the miles.

Scene

Beats, goals, and emotional turns laid out so drafting has somewhere to land.

Draft

Prose in chunks with summaries and entity awareness so cast and places stay coherent.

Polish

Edit passes that respect your voice. You approve what stays.

Export

Markdown you can keep editing. EPUB and DOCX when the manuscript is ready to leave the nest.

Minimum requirements

The machine that can finish a book

Windows is the path we support for a full install. A full novel can take hours on one PC. Small tests are quicker.

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
  • 16 GB of RAM for a real book run. 8 GB can limp on a tiny model.
  • A modern CPU. An NVIDIA GPU with about 8 GB of VRAM makes local models much faster.
  • Free disk for the app and for Ollama models. Big models need more room.
  • Ollama, installed by you, talking to this computer.
  • Python 3.11 until a frozen installer hides that step.

You do not need a cloud account to draft locally. Mac and Linux exist for people already comfortable building the workshop themselves. They are not the supported install.
