Local first Alpha to beta path

Your novel, your machine, your pace.

Novelmate Studio is a local first workspace for long form fiction: planning, drafting, polish, and export. We do not pretend to be a hosted factory. Full book runs can take hours on a single workstation. We say that up front.

Shipped direction

The product aims at a clear pipeline: premise and outline in, structured prose out, exports such as EPUB and DOCX when the toolchain on your side is configured. Exact formats and GUI surfaces evolve; check the release notes in the application repo when you install.

Roadmap

Hosted collaboration, unlimited cloud throughput, and “one click bestseller” claims are not on the honesty list. If it is not measured on your hardware, we do not sell it as done.

Who it is for

  • Authors who want custody of drafts and models on their own systems.
  • Writers who are fine with queue style runs instead of magic instant books.
  • Teams evaluating subscription or pilot access as we wire billing (see Pricing).
