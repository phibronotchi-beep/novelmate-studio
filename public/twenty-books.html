Twenty books

I am selecting 20 adults. You send one book idea and an author photo. I run that idea through Novelmate Studio under my direction, and I hand you a finished manuscript you own.

When it opens

This offer opens Tuesday, September 29, 2026, at 8 in the morning, Edmonton time. The form stays closed until then. You can read the rules now.

Who can send an idea

  • You are 18 or older.
  • One book per person.
  • I choose the 20. It is not a race, and it is not a public vote.
  • When 20 people are chosen, the offer closes.

What you send

  • Your name, or the author name you want on the book.
  • An email I can reply to.
  • The idea, in a short paragraph.
  • Any tone or genre notes you care about.
  • A photo of you that you have the right to use. Clear enough to put in the front of the book.

What you get

  • The manuscript in Markdown, EPUB, and DOCX.
  • Your photo in the front matter.
  • Ordinary front matter and back matter.
  • A short note that you own the finished files.

What this offer does not include

  • An illustrated cover.
  • An ISBN.
  • Help publishing or selling the book.
  • A print copy or an audiobook.
  • More creative rewrites after delivery. If the pipeline introduces a clear factual error, I will correct that. I will not keep rewriting the book.

How long it takes

Several weeks, in batches of 3 to 5. Not a few days. Novelmate Studio is still in the workshop. You do not have to buy it. This is a demonstration with a person directing the work. There is no promise of sales, rankings, or a publishing deal.

Ownership

When I deliver the files, you own that manuscript. You may publish it, sell it, change it, or give it away. I may say that this offer happened. I will not name you in public unless you say I can. Your photo is for your book, and for any later use you agree to in writing.

After you send it

I write to the people I choose, with the timing and a yes I need back before I start. If I do not choose your idea, I send a short thank you once the 20 spots are filled.

I keep your note, your email, and your photo so I can make the book and remember what we agreed. I do not put them on the site.

These are the rules of this offer. They are plain language from me, not a lawyer's opinion.
